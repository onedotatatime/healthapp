# Pathien Vault: device spec (CONCEPT, not built)

Concept render only. No hardware exists. Images are AI-generated product photography; the engraved mark and status dot are composited from the real `docs/brand/logo/pathien-symbol.svg` geometry.

- **Form:** a smooth, slim squircle "river pebble", soft-edged on every side, one seamless body. Fits a palm, a pocket or a keyring (detachable loop, not shown).
- **Size:** about 64 x 52 x 12 mm, about 45 g. Concept target, not engineered.
- **Material:** anodised aluminium shell, bead-blasted matte, with a fine polished chamfer where the top meets the sides.
- **Colourways:** teal-night (#0B3B3D, hero) and air-white (#F7FAFA). Same form, same mark.
- **Brand mark:** the Pathien "p and path" is laser-etched tone-on-tone, shallow and quiet. It ends in the coral dot.
- **Status light:** the dot (coral #F4846B) is an inset LED behind a flush enamel lens. It is the only light on the device.
  - Steady: record is safe and idle.
  - Slow pulse: a doctor has been let in by QR on the phone.
  - Off: asleep.
- **Interface:** none on the device. The phone app pairs, unlocks and lets a doctor in by QR.
- **Deliberately absent:** screen, buttons, ports and slots visible from outside (charging is wireless pad or contact base), medical cross, circuitry or "tech" styling, any text beyond the mark, plastic finish.
- **Story it tells:** the patient's encrypted record lives in an object they own, at home or on a keyring. Calm, personal, premium.
