**Not legal advice and not a clearance.** The trademark rows come from TMview, which says of itself that it is not an official register. A trademark attorney has to run the clearance before any public launch. Nothing was registered, bought, claimed or signed up for.

## Verdict: caution

**What is clear**

- No trade mark "PATHIEN" in any office TMview covers (EUIPO, USPTO, UK, Greece and the rest).
- No company, no LinkedIn company page and no app called Pathien.
- `pathien.health`, `pathien.app` and `getpathien.com` have no registration record.

**What needs care**

1. **The handle `pathien` is taken on Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook**, all by private people. One French blogger has used "Pathien" as his name online since 2007 and owns `pathien.com` (registered 2009). He is not a business and not a trade mark conflict, but the plain handle and the .com are gone. `getpathien` and `pathienhealth` returned no profile on Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube and GitHub.
2. **"Pathien" is the word for God in Hmar**, a language of Northeast India. It is not offensive. It is a sacred word, and Hmar Christian texts fill part of the search results for it. It matters if the product ever goes to India.
3. **Close names in the United States.** PATHIZE (pending application, classes 9 and 42, the same kind of product), PATHIAN (registered, classes 35 and 36), PATHEN MEDICAL (registered, class 10), and Pathient Corporation in Massachusetts. In the EU, UK and Greece nothing close turned up in classes 9, 42 or 44.
4. **The word sits one letter from "patient".** Google treats "pathien" as an unknown word and shows look-alikes. People who hear the name will type "patient". An examiner may also ask whether a word this close to "patient" is distinctive for a patient app. That is a question for the attorney, not one this check can answer.

## Next steps for the founder

1. **Register the domains today:** `pathien.health`, `pathien.app`, `getpathien.com`. Check `pathien.gr` and `pathien.eu` at the registrar and take them if they are free.
2. **Claim one handle everywhere:** `getpathien` (it matches `getpathien.com`) on Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn and GitHub. The plain `pathien` is gone on five of them, so use the same fallback on all seven.
3. **Before any public launch, pay a trademark attorney for a clearance, then file PATHIEN at the EUIPO in classes 9, 42 and 44.** Ask two questions: is a word this close to "patient" distinctive enough, and does the pending PATHIZE application matter if the product enters the United States.
