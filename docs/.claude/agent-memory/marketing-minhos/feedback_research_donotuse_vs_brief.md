On the healthapp deck (MIN-4429, 2026-11-13) the brief asked for TAM/SAM/SOM while Sales' research listed "our TAM, SAM and SOM themselves" under do-not-use until price and share are validated. I kept the slide with "assumption" on every dollar figure, put the verified count (AAMC 905,452 US direct-care doctors) forward, and raised it as PRD Q24 for the CEO.

**Why:** the founder's standing rule is never invent or overstate a number; the coordinator's brief is still the task. Labelling plus a named decision owner satisfies both and does not block design.

**How to apply:** for any deck, read the research's verified / secondary / do-not-use marks first; slides carry only verified numbers; anything else is either labelled on the slide itself or goes to the open-questions table with an owner. Also: an issue's acceptance criteria can disagree with the dispatch brief (here "last slide is regulatory" vs "regulatory then team and ask") — write both versions and name who decides.
