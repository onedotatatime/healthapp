5 · Business Model Canvas

Pilot-first, provider-paid, free for patients. Test a per-case fee on one international-patient department first. The clinician and the regulatory opinion are the first two costs.

Key partners

Clinician, hospital pilot, lab or rail for "verified by source", patient group, regulatory adviser. None signed.

Key activities

Measure accuracy on real paper; consented share and log; stay on the safe side of the device line; DPIA.

Key resources

Clinical lead (missing); regulatory and DP competence (missing); zero-retention model access; trust.

Value propositions

Doctor: cited answers. Buyer: sourced history at the desk ("before arrival" to test). Patient: photograph once; you decide who sees it.

Relationships

Pilot with a named clinical lead; concierge onboarding through the coordinator.

Channels

The asker: a department or clinic asks its patients. No consumer acquisition. Nothing contacted.

Customer segments

First: international-patient departments. Next: clinic chains, telehealth, labs. Patients and doctors never pay. Five insurer edges: open, not placed.

Cost structure

Record-only pilot, one EU cell: 2–3 engineers, part-time security, a clinical lead, 4–6 months, legal in the low tens of thousands of euros (CTO estimate). Certification only if the assistant ships: class IIa about €300k–800k, 18–30 months.

Revenue streams

Per-case fee (test first); per-doctor seat (test second); lab partnership; grants as funding. No price sourced. No data sale.
