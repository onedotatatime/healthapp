## What the room agrees on

1. The first buyer to test is a hospital's **international-patient department** (four seats reached it alone; the CTO names its cost).
2. **"Possible causes" stays out** of every proposition until certification is funded and a clinician exists.
3. **No dollar TAM, SAM or SOM on a slide** until a buyer names a figure.
4. **The weakest rows are willingness to pay, demand evidence, distribution and team.** The problem is the only strong row.
5. **Never "the only" or "the first"**; do not compete on "AI reads your exam".
