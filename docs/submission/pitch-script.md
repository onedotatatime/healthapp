# Pitch script: 3 minutes, Pathien (MIN-4524; v3 name and revenue line, MIN-4503)

Semifinal, about Sun 19:00. Judging: usefulness, craft, aura. Run the live demo; if a step fails, switch to the screenshots and narrate, do not retry. Everything on screen is the working demo, on synthetic patients. Times are cumulative. Product name: **Pathien** (trademark clearance pending; no ™ on screen). Page: https://healthapp-landing.pages.dev. Numbers match `docs/deck/deck-content.md` v3.1 (team and ask: slides 14 and 15).

## 0:00 to 0:25. The problem

"This is Maria. She has knee pain. At night she asks a search engine what it means. By day she sees two doctors, and each sees only the page she remembered to bring. Her history is a folder of paper. Up to 15 percent of diagnoses are inaccurate, delayed or wrong. Pathien gives the doctor the whole history, in seconds."

## 0:25 to 0:55. Scan and check

*Screen: Maria photographs a blood test.*
"Maria photographs an exam. About 17 seconds later it is values, dates, units and ranges, and she checks every one before it is saved. On our synthetic test set all 235 of 235 fields were read correctly."
*Screen: she adds her husband's exam.*
"Her husband's exam: refused. Not her name, not her ID."

## 0:55 to 1:25. The handshake

*Screen: DNA code on her phone, doctor scans, she approves.*
"At the orthopaedist she shows a code shaped like DNA. He scans, she approves. The doctor's view opens in 3.6 seconds, with a timer. In person, with her yes, for a set time. The database refuses anyone outside that grant."

## 1:25 to 2:10. The doctor asks

*Screen: "Has her CRP been raised before?"*
"He asks: has her CRP been raised before? About 15 seconds later: dates and values, five sources, each one a link to the exam it came from. The assistant answers only from her record. If it is not there, it says so."
*Screen: possibilities view.*
"He can ask for possibilities. They are labelled for the doctor to evaluate, with their sources, and never shown to Maria. The patient never gets a diagnosis from us."

## 2:10 to 2:30. It ends

*Screen: time runs out.*
"Time ends. The doctor's screen clears in about 3 seconds. Maria's log shows who looked and until when. The log holds fingerprints and access events, never health data. No insurer ever gets in."

## 2:30 to 3:00. Honest close, who pays, and the ask

"Real: reading, identity match, time-limited access, the signed log. Simulated tonight: the key on our server, our own verified lab, seeded matches. Hospitals pay per case and clinics per seat, never per diagnosis: half a million to five million euros a year by year three, on our assumptions. I am Lefteris Georgaras, founder of onedotatatime.design. I ask for six hundred thousand euros over eighteen months, released only when a hospital signs a pilot. Pathien. Maria should not have to ask a search engine."

## Likely questions (answers from the record)

- Is it a medical device? Possibly, for the doctor-side possibilities. The regulatory path (EU MDR, GDPR, EHDS) is the closing slide of the deck, and nothing is certified.
- Real patients or pilots? None. Synthetic data, nobody contacted.
- How do you make money? Hospitals pay per case (international-patient departments, €40 to €80 a case) and clinic chains per doctor seat (€50 a month). Year three: about €0.56M to €4.85M. Every price and volume is an assumption the pilots test; the patient is free; insurers never. We never earn from a diagnosis because we never make one. (Deck slide 11.)
- Is that a fraction of the savings? Slide 8 puts the saving on tests at €4M to €75M a year in Greece alone; our year-3 revenue from every country is about 6 to 14% of it. Our estimate.
- Who is the team, and what do you ask? Today the founder, Lefteris Georgaras (onedotatatime.design), with AI agents. The ask is €600k over 18 months, our plan: €200k when a hospital agrees a pilot in writing, €400k only when the pilot proves €40 a case or more, 1,000 cases a year or more and €5 cost per case or less. It funds four hires (engineer, ML engineer, business development, marketing) and a research partner, a university hospital and an AI research centre, targets not yet contacted. 28% goes to selling. Nothing is raised before the pilot. (Deck slides 14 and 15.)
- Blockchain? No. A signed, hash-chained log of fingerprints and grants; health data on a chain would conflict with GDPR erasure.
