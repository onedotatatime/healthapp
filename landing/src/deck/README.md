# Pitch deck (served at `/deck`)

A web presentation built from reusable slide templates. The words live in one data file.

- `slides.ts`: the whole deck as typed data. One object per slide. Words come from `docs/deck/deck-content.md` (v4.3). Main slides 1 to 14 plus 5b, 6b, 6c; then the appendix after slide 14. Speaker notes are in `notes.ts`, appendix pages in `appendix.ts` (A7 is in `slides.ts`).
- `types.ts`: the props every template takes (a `Slide` is a union keyed by `template`).
- `templates/`: layouts. `Cover`, `Hero` (full-bleed image + text column + optional `tag` of `'Concept render'`, optional `stepsTitle` + `steps` (a small numbered "how it's used" list, numbers in teal); used by slide 5b), `Stats`, `Statement` (points + side panel), `Funnel` (big number), `Chart` (bars from data: one series = conservative teal and upside mint with only the totals; several series stack; one second number under the chart), `Table`, `Product` (phone frames), `Compare` (positioning map and a short callout), `Steps` (timeline or ascending cards, or `variant: 'flow'`: a quiet left-to-right four-step flow with a `body` paragraph, small teal numerals on a hairline, the last step ending on a coral dot, no image; no slide uses it since v3.9), `TwoColumn` (`template: 'twocol'`: a headline, two titled columns of numbered short lines, column 1 ending on the coral dot, and an optional small `pointer` line; no image; used by slide 6b), `ThreeCards` (`template: 'threecards'`: a headline and three equal cards, each a caps title, one big figure with a small `mark` ("*") on it, one `unit` explanation line, and optional extra `lines` where `[[...]]` renders a teal label; `accent: true` on a card gives it the light teal band and a teal outline; no image; used by slide 6c), `TeamAsk`, `Appendix` (A7: nested circles and a table), `Spend` (`template: 'spend'`: one headline, one line, one big bar in four parts with the accent on the first, the contact line and QR; used by slide 14), `Detail` (`template: 'detail'`: an appendix page, a compact title and one to three columns of a small markdown subset: `**bold**`, `*label*`, `- ` and `1. ` lists, `| tables |`, a lone bold line as a sub-heading; `grid` sets the columns, `fs` the type size in px, default 19; used by A1 to A9 except A7).
- `Deck.tsx`: the presenter (scaling, keys, hash, notes, print). `deck.css`: all styles, on a fixed 1920x1080 canvas.
- Entry: `landing/deck/index.html` (`noindex`), built to `dist/deck/index.html`. The landing at `/` is a separate entry.

## Make a new deck

1. Copy `slides.ts` to `slides.<name>.ts`, keep the `Slide[]` shape, and replace the data. Pick a `template` per slide; TypeScript tells you which fields it needs.
2. Point `main.tsx` at the new file (or add `deck/<name>/index.html` and a second input in `vite.config.ts`).
3. Rules baked into the types: a `product` card and a `statement` side panel of kind `notice`, `phone` or `matches` may carry an optional `tag`, and the only one in use is `'Working demo · synthetic data'` (no slide uses it since the live-demo slide was removed in v4.2). The `'Mock-up'` tag no longer exists: the founder waived the concept and mock-up labels on 14 and 15 Nov 2026. A slide of the `stats` template may omit `chips` and `closing`. Do not add red, a trademark sign, or a funding-sources slide.
4. A `hero` slide's `tag`, `kicker`, `stepsTitle` and `steps` are optional. Slide 5b carries no concept label (founder, 2026-11-15: it is no longer shown as a concept); a hero that does show an unbuilt thing should still say so with a `tag`.
5. Estimates: a small "*" on the number and one footnote line (`note`); see "v4.3" below.

## Presenting

Arrows, space, Enter, PageUp/Down, click (left third goes back); Home/End; `F` fullscreen; `N` speaker notes. The URL hash is the slide `id`, not its position: `/deck#8`, `#5b` (the Vault), `#6b` (clinical trials), `#6c` (its economics), `#A3` (an appendix slide). A slide with a `counter` (5b, 6b, 6c and the appendix) shows that label and is not counted, so the others keep "n / 14". Print (Cmd+P, save as PDF) gives one 16:9 slide per page. Screenshots: `?clean&scale1` hides the presenter chrome and pins the slide at 1920x1080.

## v4.3: appendix links, footnotes, new templates

- **Estimates.** An estimate carries a small "\*" on its number (`rich()` sets a lone `*` or `†` as a small raised mark; a card figure takes `mark: '*'`) and ONE small footnote line, the slide's `note` (19px, ink-soft, bottom-left). It replaces the old same-size "(our estimate)" label, so `.tc-tag` / `figure.tag` are gone. `footer` (24px) stays for statements that must stay readable ("Named organisations are targets", "Consent only"). On the appendix pages the labels stay in full next to each input.
- **Links.** `links: [{ to: 'A2', label: 'Details → A2' }]` on a main slide renders hash links bottom-right, left of the lockup. `back: [{ to: '4', label: 'Back to slide 4' }]` on an appendix slide renders them top-right. They are plain `<a href="#A2">`: Tab and Enter work, and `Deck.tsx` leaves Enter and Space to a focused link or button; the arrow keys still change slides, and the focus is released after the jump so the arrows work straight away.
- **Hashes and counters.** Main slides `#1` to `#14`, `#5b`, `#6b`, `#6c` (counters "n / 14", "5b", "6b", "6c"). Appendix `#A1` to `#A9` (counters "A1" ...). A split appendix block has consecutive slides: the first keeps the bare id (`#A2`, counter "A2a"), the next are `#A2b`, `#A2c` (A2, A3, A6 and A9 are split). `#a` and `#appendix` open A1. Unknown hashes open slide 1.
- **Print.** Cmd+P prints every slide, main and appendix, one 16:9 page each (33 pages); links print as text.
- **Order.** Defined in `slides.ts`: main slides, then A1 to A9 in order (`appendix` list).
