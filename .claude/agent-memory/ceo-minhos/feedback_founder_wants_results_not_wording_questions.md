On the health app hackathon demo, bring the founder results he can see, not wording-level approvals. On 2026-11-14, asked to approve the simulation's assumption sentence (MIN-4506), he answered: "not my decisio. I want results and somehow to visualise them" (relayed by the coordinator, not heard directly).

**Why:** He presents at a hackathon with a hard deadline (Sun 15 Nov 2026, 18:00 Athens) and his lean rule is in force. A sentence-level question costs him attention and gives him nothing to look at.

**How to apply:** When a demo decision is small, reversible and already framed honestly (a labelled simulation, a stated assumption), expect him to hand it over. Decide it as "CEO on the founder's behalf", say that he delegated it and in which words, log it in conductor's `docs/founder-decisions.md`, and report the outcome in one line (the screen, the numbers), not a memo. Each delegation covers its own case only: anything that leaves the labelled demo (a deck slide, a post, a site) or reads as a clinical-benefit claim still goes to him, as conductor's rules say.
